# Access, Not Ownership -- current-paper Lean 4 formalization

The publication root formalizes the live Regulation & Governance manuscript,
not the historical extended-paper theorem inventory.

## Current status

- 26/26 numbered formal objects are present in the proof-carrying ledger.
- 20/20 displayed formulas have adjacent TeX-to-Lean bindings.
- 11 derived mathematical entries are kernel proved.
- Definitions and maintained assumptions are typed at their manuscript role.
- The current-paper namespace has no `axiom`, `opaque`, `sorry`, `admit`, or
  `native_decide` declaration.
- The axiom audit reports only `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound`.

The former Gini, AntiTipping, and multi-agency modules remain historical
extensions. They are not imported by `AccessOrthogonality.lean` and do not
count toward current-paper coverage.

## Reproduction

From this repository root:

```powershell
lake build AccessOrthogonality AccessOrthogonality.Ledger
lake env lean AccessOrthogonality/CurrentPaperAxiomAudit.lean
lake env lean AccessOrthogonality/CurrentPaperLedger.lean
```

The publication root imports only the current-paper theorem map. The axiom
audit covers every derived endpoint, including the full two-stage bundled-game
deviation path generated by one fixed tie-break. The ledger reports 26 objects,
11 derived entries, and zero unfinished entries.

The canonical paper and its project-owned TeX-to-Lean/Paper Infrastructure
contracts remain in the manuscript repository; this public companion contains
the independently buildable Lean kernel surface used by the submitted paper.
